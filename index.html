Welcome to Rajpalwan

A village with roots, people and possibilities

Rajpalwan, also written as Raj Palwan, is a village in Fatehpur Tehsil of Kangra district, Himachal Pradesh, India. This website is intended as a shared place for residents, families living away from home, visitors and anyone who wants to learn about the village.

Village at a glance

  • Village: Rajpalwan / Raj Palwan
  • Tehsil: Fatehpur
  • District: Kangra
  • State: Himachal Pradesh, India
  • PIN code: 176058
  • Administrative context: Local governance through the applicable Gram Panchayat and district administration

Build the village together

A practical vision for Rajpalwan

A community-led list of ideas that residents can discuss, prioritise and take forward with the appropriate local authorities.

Quality of life priorities

  • Safe, well-maintained roads and drainage
  • Reliable drinking water and clean surroundings
  • Good learning opportunities for every child
  • Awareness of healthcare and emergency services
  • Waste segregation, cleanliness and environmental care
  • Safe public spaces for children, women and older residents

Opportunity for the next generation

  • Digital literacy and access to online public services
  • Career guidance, skills training and job information
  • Support for local entrepreneurs and small businesses
  • Sports, cultural activities and youth participation
  • Recognition of students, achievers and community contributors
  • A documented village history for future generations
